## The games

<div class="wg-card-grid">
  <HubCard game="polygram" title="Polygram" href="/docs/games/polygram/"
    accent="light-dark(#b91c1c, #f87171)">
    Spell your way from triangle to decagon. Each polygon level asks for every
    word of its size — find them all and a new letter joins.
  </HubCard>
  <HubCard game="crosshatch" title="Crosshatch" href="/docs/games/crosshatch/"
    accent="light-dark(#0c7d84, #3ddbd9)">
    Every way the words fit. A small crossword frame with given letters — the
    goal isn't one solution, it's all of them.
  </HubCard>
  <HubCard game="backwords" title="Backwords" href="/docs/games/backwords/"
    accent="light-dark(#a21caf, #e879f9)">
    Every word, a reflection. Place letters against a mirror; the reflection
    spells the rest.
  </HubCard>
  <HubCard game="doublet" title="Doublet" href="/docs/games/doublet/"
    accent="light-dark(#92400e, #fbbf24)">
    Place the tiles. Spell the words. Letter dominoes onto a shaped board, with
    exactly one valid arrangement.
  </HubCard>
  <HubCard game="serpentine" title="Serpentine" href="/docs/games/serpentine/"
    accent="light-dark(#4d7c0f, #a3e635)">
    One continuous line. Trace a path through every cell to reveal a hidden
    line of poetry.
  </HubCard>
  <HubCard title="Daily puzzles" href="/docs/games/daily-puzzles/">
    Every generator is a pure function of the date. Seeded determinism,
    quality gates and version stamps keep everyone's puzzle identical —
    offline, with no server.
  </HubCard>
</div>
