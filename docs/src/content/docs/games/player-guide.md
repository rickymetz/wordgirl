This page explains the parts of WordGirl around the puzzles: streaks, the other play modes, and the app itself.

## Streaks

A streak is your count of daily puzzles solved on consecutive days.

- A solve advances your streak when you solve today's daily puzzle.
- Some games give you more than one board a day: Crosshatch has a Normal and a Hard board, Serpentine has a haiku and a poem, and Doublet has three sizes. For those games the day counts when you solve **every** board, and the streak advances then — the same moment the hub card calls the day finished.
- There is a margin after midnight: in the short time after the date changes, a solve of yesterday's puzzle still counts. A slow evening game does not break your streak at 12:01.
- If a day passes with no solve, your streak shows zero again. Your best streak stays in your statistics.
- Archive plays never advance a streak. Only the daily puzzle counts.

## The archive

Each game keeps every day you played. Open a game's archive from its hub card:

- A calendar shows your played and solved days.
- You can open and play a past day's puzzle. It is the same puzzle everyone had on that date.
- Archive solves count in your statistics, but they do not advance streaks.
- A replay of a finished day asks for confirmation first.

## Practice mode

Each game has a practice mode with a random puzzle instead of the daily one. Practice is unlimited — play as many as you want. Practice results do not enter your statistics, and practice has no share button.

## The stats page

Each game has a stats page with small charts: one chart for each metric over the last 30 days, and a chart of your usual solve hour. Touch a day on a chart to see its value. A gap in a chart is a day without data — the charts never show a false zero.

## Install the app

WordGirl is a web app that you can install:

- **iPhone and iPad**: open wordgirl.net in Safari, touch Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
- **Android**: open wordgirl.net in Chrome and accept the install prompt, or use "Add to Home screen" in the menu.
- **Desktop**: use the install icon in the address bar.

## Play offline

The app is fully playable without a connection. Your device makes the daily puzzles itself — refer to [How daily puzzles work](/docs/games/daily-puzzles/). The word list and the app are stored on your device after the first visit.

Your progress also stays on your device. There is no account and no server copy. If you remove the app and its site data, your history is gone — the app asks the browser for persistent storage to protect against automatic cleanup.

## Settings

The settings dialog (the gear on the hub) has:

- **Theme**: system, light, or dark.
- **Text size**: 87.5 to 125 percent. The game boards scale with it.
- **Check for updates**: the app updates itself each hour; this button checks immediately.
