Polygram is a word game. You start with a triangle of three letters. You make words from these letters. When you find all the words, a new letter is added. The triangle becomes a square. The largest shape is a decagon with ten letters.

## How to play

1. Look at the letters on the polygon.
2. Make a word that has the same number of letters as the polygon. You can use each letter more than one time.
3. Find all the necessary words to go to the next level.

Bonus words are unusual words. They increase your score. They are not necessary for the next level.

If you cannot find a word, you can show a hint letter. Each hint letter divides the points for that word by two. The minimum is one point.

Your score for a word is the number of its letters. Each level that you complete gives bonus points. The bonus is the number of letters in the level. The maximum score includes all necessary words and all bonus words without hints.

## How the game makes a puzzle

The generator makes the puzzle in these steps:

1. The generator selects a random word with three letters from the dictionary. The different letters of this word are the start letters. If the word has fewer than three different letters, the generator adds letters from a list of frequent letters.
2. The generator finds all the words with three letters that the start letters can make. The triangle level must have between 1 and 8 words. Each start letter must be in one word or more.
3. The generator tries to add one letter for each level from 4 to 10. A new letter is correct only when:
   - The number of words is in the limits for that level.
   - The new letter is in one word or more.
4. The generator keeps the puzzle only when:
   - The puzzle has five levels or more.
   - The puzzle has a maximum of 35 necessary words.

   If the puzzle is not correct, the generator starts again. The maximum is 300 tries.

The word limits for each level are in this table:

| Level size | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 |
|-----------|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|----|
| Maximum words | 8 | 10 | 10 | 8 | 6 | 5 | 4 | 3 |

Levels 7 to 10 must have a minimum of two words. One word is not sufficient for these levels. With only one word, the player sees no progress until the full word is found.

## The mathematics

### The bitmask test

Each word in the dictionary has a mask of 26 bits. Each bit shows one letter of the alphabet. A word is possible when its mask is a subset of the puzzle mask. The test is one AND operation:

$$
(m_w \;\land\; \lnot\, m_p) = 0
$$

Here $m_w$ is the mask of the word. $m_p$ is the mask of the puzzle letters. Thus the test for one word takes constant time. The search for a full level is one scan of one length group. The code is in `src/lib/words/dictionary.ts`.

### The hint formula

Each hint letter divides the points for a word by two. The result cannot go below one point:

$$
\text{points}(w, h) = \max\!\left(1,\; \left\lfloor \frac{|w|}{2^{h}} \right\rfloor \right)
$$

Here $|w|$ is the number of letters in the word. $h$ is the number of hint letters.

### The search method

The generator is a greedy hill climber. It adds one letter at each step. It never removes a letter. A bad letter sequence fails the quality limits. Then the full puzzle is rejected, and a new random start begins. This method is rejection sampling. It is simple and fast because each single test is cheap.

The level limits also have a mathematical purpose. The number of possible words grows with the number of letters. The limits keep each level small. Thus the task "find all the words" stays possible for a person, not only for a computer.
